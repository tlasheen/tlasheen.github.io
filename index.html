Presentlee

Lecture Recorder for iPhone and Android

Record a lecture or talk slide by slide: a photo or short video of each slide, with your voice. Presentlee turns it into a narrated PowerPoint you can share.

  • Photo or video for each slide, with your voice recorded alongside
  • Scan text: copy printed text from a photo into the notes (English and Arabic)
  • Voice to notes, done on your phone
  • Watch presentations in the app, organise them in categories
  • Export as PowerPoint in Original, Medium or Small size
  • No account, and your recordings stay on your phone
