Presentlee Privacy Policy

Applies to Presentlee - Lecture Recorder on iPhone and Android.
Last updated: 25 November 2026

In short: your photos, videos, voice recordings, notes and presentations stay on your phone. We have no server and no account system, and we never receive your content. The free version shows Google AdMob ads, and Google collects some device data to show them. Purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

1. Who we are

Presentlee is published by Tarek Lasheen, Rehab City, Egypt (the "controller"). Contact for privacy questions: tlasheen@gmail.com.

2. What the app does

Presentlee records lectures and presentations slide by slide. You take photos or record videos and your voice is recorded for each slide. You can read printed text from a photo into the slide notes, turn your voice into notes, watch your presentations in the app, and export them as PowerPoint files. The app works without an account.

4. Permissions

  • Camera: to take photos and record videos for your slides.
  • Microphone: to record your voice for each slide. While recording, Android shows a notification so you know the microphone is in use and so the phone does not stop the recording.
  • Notifications (Android): for that recording notification.
  • Battery optimisation (Android): the app may ask to be left out of battery saving so a long recording is not cut off. You can refuse.
  • Photo library (iPhone): the app does not read your photo library. Photos and videos come from the camera inside the app.
  • Tracking (iPhone): see section 6.

You can refuse or withdraw any permission in your phone's settings. Some features will then not work.

5. Text scanning and voice to notes

Scan text reads printed text from your photos on your phone, using Google ML Kit text recognition (Latin script) and Tesseract (Arabic). Photos are not sent anywhere to be read. On Android, the page scanner uses Google Play services on your phone.

Voice to notes turns the voice of each slide into text entirely on your phone, with a speech model that is part of the app. Your voice is not sent to us, Google, Apple or anyone else.

Google may collect technical data about how its on-phone components (ML Kit, Play services) perform, under Google's own policies.

6. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The free version shows a short rewarded video ad from Google AdMob before an export, after your first 5 free exports. To show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID, or Apple's IDFA when you allow tracking),
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information, and how you interact with ads.

Google acts as an independent controller for this data: see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

  • iPhone: the app asks through Apple's "Allow tracking" pop-up. With your permission, Google AdMob uses your advertising identifier to show more relevant ads. If you say no, ads are still shown but are not personalised with your advertising identifier. The app itself does not collect any other personal data. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Europe, UK and Switzerland: Google's consent message asks for your choice before ads are personalised. You can change it later with the "Ad choices" button on the app's Remove ads page.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's Google settings > Ads.

7. Sponsor slides

Exported PowerPoint files from the free version may contain a sponsor slide (a picture, with no tracking in it). The app downloads the list of sponsor pictures from a public file we publish on GitHub. This is a plain web request that carries no identifier and nothing about you; like any web server, GitHub sees your IP address. Which picture is used can depend on your phone's region and language settings; this choice is made on your phone, and the app never reads your location.

8. Purchases

"Remove ads" passes (1 month or 1 year) are sold through the Apple App Store or Google Play. The store handles the payment; we never receive your card or payment details. The app only learns that a pass was bought, to switch ads off for that period. On iPhone, to restore a pass after a reinstall or on a new iPhone, the app reads your purchase history for Presentlee from the App Store on your phone.

9. Contacting us

If you tap "Write to us" on the Contact screen, your e-mail app opens with a short note about the app version, system, phone region and language. You can read and change it before sending; we receive it only if you send it. We use e-mails only to answer you.

10. What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no server of ours.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service of ours.
  • We do not sell your data.

11. Legal bases (EEA, UK)

  • Features you use (recording, scanning, exporting): performance of a contract. This data stays on your phone.
  • Personalised ads and advertising identifiers: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.
  • Non-personalised ads that keep the app free: our legitimate interests, where the law allows.
  • Purchases: performance of a contract, handled by the store.

12. Keeping and deleting data

Your presentations stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall the app. We hold no copy. Data held by Google, Apple or the stores follows their own retention rules.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or California law), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or move your data, to object to or restrict its use, and to withdraw consent. Your content is only on your phone, so you control it directly in the app. For data held by Google or Apple, use their privacy tools. For anything about Presentlee, write to tlasheen@gmail.com. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

14. International transfers

Google, Apple and the stores may process data in other countries, including the United States, under their own safeguards. We do not transfer your presentations anywhere.

15. Children

Presentlee is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has used the app, you can delete it and contact us.

16. Security

Your content is kept in the app's private storage, protected by your phone's own security. Files leave the app only when you share them.

17. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
