Presentlee Support

Presentlee - Lecture Recorder turns a lecture or talk into a narrated PowerPoint. Take a photo of each slide or board, or record a short video, and your voice is recorded with it. Read printed text from photos into the notes, turn your voice into notes, watch the presentation in the app, and export it as a PowerPoint file to share. Everything stays on your phone, in English and Arabic.

Need help? Write to us, and include your phone model and what happened. In the app, Menu > Contact > "Write to us" fills in the app version for you.

Common questions

How do I record a presentation?

Tap the orange + button. Tap Photo to take a picture of the slide (or Video to film), and speak: your voice is recorded for that slide until you take the next photo. Tap Stop when you are done and choose a category to save it in.

How do I read text from a photo?

Tap Photo, then "Scan text": the app takes the picture and copies its printed text into the slide's notes. It reads Latin-script languages (such as English) and Arabic, on your phone.

How do I export and share?

Open the menu > My presentations, pick the category, then tap the export button of a presentation. Choose a quality (Original, Medium or Small) and send the PowerPoint file with any app. Tick "Transcribe voice to notes" to add what you said to the notes (English).

Why does a video play before an export?

Your first 5 exports are free. After that, a short video ad plays before each export, which keeps the app free. A Remove ads pass (1 month or 1 year) removes the videos and sponsor slides, and lets you export several presentations at once.

I bought Remove ads. How do I get it back on a new phone or after reinstalling?

iPhone: open Menu > Remove ads and tap "Restore purchases", signed in with the same Apple ID. The app also checks by itself when it starts. Android: with Google backup on, the ad-free date comes back with the app; if it does not, write to us with your Google Play order number.

Where are my presentations stored? Are they backed up?

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage. We have no server and cannot see or recover them. Uninstalling the app deletes them, so export the ones you want to keep first.

The recording stopped when I left the app (Android).

Allow notifications for Presentlee and, when asked, let it ignore battery saving. Some phones also have their own battery settings for each app: set Presentlee to "unrestricted".

How do I change my ad privacy choices?

iPhone: Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Europe and UK: the "Ad choices" button on the app's Remove ads page. Android: your phone's Google settings > Ads. See the privacy policy.
